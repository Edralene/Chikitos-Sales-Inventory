Data Erase & Reset

Admin-only cleanup. You can erase a specific date, selectively reset records, or wipe all store activity including Master Audit.
DESTRUCTIVE
1. Specific DatePreview and remove records tied to one date.
Choose a date, then click Preview This Date.
Selective ResetChoose only specific record groups
Erase EVERYTHING for This Date Deletes all daily transactions, credit payments, expenses, requests, closings, shifts, purchases, stock-movement history, legacy restocks, deleted-sale archive entries, and Reset History tied to the selected date. Master Audit entries for that date are removed.
NO AUDIT LEFT
Specific Date: Product Master, Processor list, and System Settings are preserved. Stock is reconciled before daily records are removed. A full-date erase does not create a new Reset History entry, so the selected date can disappear from Master Audit.

Erase ALL Store Data

Hard wipe for testing or starting fresh. This clears all operational collections and Master Audit across every date.
HIGH RISK
Preview exact Firebase record counts before erasing.
Erase All includes Master Audit. Sales, Credits, Payments, Expenses, Stock Movement History, Void/Delete/Return requests, Deleted Sales Archive, Closings, Duty/Shift history, Purchases, Reset History, Legacy Restocks, and current Inventory balances will be cleared. Login accounts and Admin/Staff roles are intentionally NOT deleted.

Reset History

Selective resets only. Full-date erase and Erase All intentionally remove audit/reset history.
